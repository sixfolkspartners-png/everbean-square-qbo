Engine finding — reconcile to Reporting + payout, never to raw orders
The Orders API returned only 410 revenue orders / $7,667.61 for these two days, but the Reporting Sales view shows 418 orders / $7,785.23 — the two Square APIs disagree by $117.62 (8 orders the Orders endpoint dropped). The missing orders are all card: adding them back puts net card sales at $7,187.98, which nets to the actual $6,962.41 payout at a 3.14% fee — right in line with the other days this week. So the day totals here are anchored to the Reporting Sales view and the CC deposit to the actual payout, with the tender split taken from orders. This is now a rule in the build spec: orders give you the tender split, but the day's totals must foot to Reporting and the payout.

Correction & engine finding — trust the reconciliation/payout, never the Orders API
Earlier I flagged "$62.71 of late card sales awaiting settlement." That was wrong — those 5 fee-free payments were the day's gift-card redemptions (Square files them under source CARD with brand SQUARE_GIFT_CARD; stored-value never carries a processing fee and never settles to the bank). There is no settlement lag — all card charges settled into the same-day payout. Your reconciliation proves the real number: card collected $2,003.24, not the Orders API's $2,067.26 — the Orders endpoint overstated card by $64.02 (an auth that never became a settled charge). Combined with the Aug 1–2 undercount, the rule is now firm: the Orders API is unreliable for tender totals — anchor the day to the reconciliation report + payout, use Orders only to attribute the cash/card proportion. Also noted for the build: Square's reporting day is a fixed 5:30 AM–6:00 PM MT window, not calendar midnight.
